# Obseri organic acquisition plan

Research and implementation: 1 November 2026. Public business name: obseri.com. Contact: ayush@flamkilabs.com.

## Positioning and evidence

Start with agencies and service businesses whose websites already receive questions about services, fit, pricing, and booking. This is a launch hypothesis to validate with customers, not a proven customer segment. The product connects website-trained chat and neural voice with consent-based enquiries, traffic context, and a shared team inbox.

Search results and competitor editorial pages show established competition around website chatbots and lead generation. Chatbase publishes lead-generation guidance; Tidio publishes sales and lead-generation material. Obseri should demonstrate its actual workflow and setup details rather than compete with generic claims about AI or publish many near-identical industry pages.

No paid keyword dataset or Google Trends time series was available for this research. The following priorities reflect intent and product fit, **not verified search volume, difficulty, or rising trends**. Search engine result position varies by location and date; this is not a rank report.

## Implemented foundation

- Server-rendered public copy, descriptive titles and descriptions, canonical URLs, social metadata, and internal links.
- Updated home page, product pages and pricing claims to reflect supported capabilities.
- A lead-generation product page and a substantive setup guide; one primary intent per page.
- Organization, website, application and page structured data where appropriate, without invented ratings or customer counts. FAQ markup is not a promise of a Google rich result.
- Sitemap containing only public canonical pages. App/auth content remains noindex; APIs and widget routes are excluded from robots crawling.
- Consistent public business contact. Customer account emails and user-provided contact information remain untouched.
- `llms.txt` as supplementary product documentation. It is not a Google indexing requirement or a ranking lever.

## Measurement and owner setup

1. Verify a Google Search Console domain property for `obseri.com` using the DNS record Google provides. Verification access must come from the property owner; do not invent a verification token.
2. Submit `https://obseri.com/sitemap.xml`. Inspect the homepage and new URLs, test their live rendering, and request indexing for those pages. Submission does not guarantee indexing or ranking.
3. Record a baseline: indexed public URLs; non-brand impressions, clicks, CTR and queries by landing page; actual organic signups; published assistants; first external enquiry; paid conversions.
4. Use first-party acquisition events to measure landing page → signup → import completed → publish → verified embed → first enquiry. Separate internal tests and founder demos from real acquisition. Never place visitor emails or message text in public analytics URLs.
5. Inspect mobile Core Web Vitals in Search Console when field data exists. Until then measure LCP, INP interactions and CLS in repeatable lab runs on a throttled mobile device. A local desktop screenshot is not a field performance score.
6. Review weekly. Diagnose impressions without clicks via intent/title alignment, clicks without signup via clarity/demo friction, and signup without publish via onboarding. Do not attribute revenue lift causally from a source association report.

Search Console verification, sitemap submission, indexing, field performance and ranking growth are external follow-up work, not completed by a code deployment.

## First 30 days

- Install the product with three willing service businesses/agencies. Use their actual content and their permission for any public examples.
- Capture the top ten questions per pilot, unsupported answers, import failures, first-response/first-audio latency, and follow-up delivery.
- Publish one detailed, permission-based installation case study after there is evidence: starting problem, configuration, screenshots, time window, traffic denominator and observed enquiries. If the result is zero enquiries, say so.
- Improve existing pages using actual Search Console queries. Fix crawl/indexing issues before adding more pages.
- Add a platform installation guide only after testing that platform. Begin with the platform used by the pilots; do not claim an unbuilt Shopify/WordPress app.

## Days 31–60

- Publish two focused guides answering recurring customer questions, such as testing source-grounded answers or configuring booking links and team handoff.
- Extend the setup guide with troubleshooting from real support requests and product changes.
- Seek relevant mentions from pilot customers and integration documentation with their approval. Avoid purchased links, fabricated reviews and mass directory submissions.
- Compare cohorts by organic landing page and activation, not traffic alone. Retain pages that solve distinct problems; consolidate overlaps.

## Days 61–90

- Add a second verified case study and a factual comparison only when competitors' current features and pricing have been checked.
- Review query clusters with impressions and activated users. Expand those clusters with original examples; do not generate dozens of thin pages.
- Reassess positioning based on who activated, received useful enquiries and paid. Set acquisition targets from that baseline rather than invented forecasts.
